Accessibility statement

Last reviewed: 12 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use accessfix.net. This website is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

How we test

Every page is tested with the axe-core engine, by keyboard only and with a screen reader before release. The typeface, Atkinson Hyperlegible, was designed for readers with low vision.

Known limitations

  • The “Shop the way your customers do” demonstration deliberately shows an inaccessible product card when “Most stores today” is selected. This is to show the problems we fix. The explanation next to it and the “After our fixes” version are fully accessible.
  • Reports describe third-party stores and quote short code snippets from them as they are.

Feedback and contact

If something on this website is hard to use, write to hello@accessfix.net. We reply within one working day and send you the content in a form you can use.
