    Why a widget on top doesn’t fix anything

    Accessibility overlay widgets add a toolbar to your store but leave the broken code underneath. Screen readers read the code, not the toolbar.

    In 2025 the US Federal Trade Commission fined the best-known overlay company $1 million for claiming its tool made websites compliant.

    Before

    <button class="header__icon">
  <svg>…</svg>
</button>

    Screen reader says “button”

    After

    <button class="header__icon"
  aria-label="Cart, 2 items">
  <svg aria-hidden="true">…</svg>
</button>

    Screen reader says “Cart, 2 items, button”

    What the European Accessibility Act means for your shop

    Since when
    28 June 2025, for online shops selling to consumers in the EU. Germany’s version is the Barrierefreiheitsstärkungsgesetz (BFSG).
    Who it covers
    Every shop except micro-businesses with fewer than 10 staff and no more than €2 million annual turnover.
    What happens if you ignore it
    National authorities can order fixes and impose fines. The amounts differ by country.

    General information, not legal advice. We fix the problems we find; we don’t certify compliance.

    Will you change my live store?

    Not without your written permission. We work on a duplicate of your theme. You preview it and publish it yourself.

    Does a fix sprint make my shop fully compliant?

    It fixes the problems we found, usually the ones that block shoppers most. Automated checks find only part of all issues, so the Full fix adds a manual keyboard and screen-reader test.

    We already use an accessibility widget. Is that enough?

    A widget doesn’t change the code underneath, so screen readers still hit the same problems. Once the real fixes are in, you can remove it.

    How long does it take?

    Most fix sprints are done within five working days after you give us access to a theme copy.
